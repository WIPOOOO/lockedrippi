Locked Rippi is a free Chrome extension. Add a site and it’s locked, subdomains included. You can allow just the subdomain you need, pause blocking whenever you like, and your original link is always saved so you can come back to it.

Privacy policy

Locked Rippi: A Website Blocker. Last updated November 21, 2026.

The short version: your data stays on your computer. I don’t collect it, sell it or share it.

What the extension looks at

To block the sites you choose, the extension compares the address of each page you open with your own list. This happens on your device, in your browser. The addresses are not saved, logged or sent anywhere.

What it stores

Your lists of blocked, allowed and paused sites. They’re kept in Chrome’s local storage on your device and are never transmitted. Deleting a site from the list, or uninstalling the extension, removes the data.

Why it asks for access to all websites

You can block any site you want, so the extension can’t know in advance which ones it will need to act on. It only acts on sites in your list, and it never reads, collects, or modifies page content.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t collect personal information.
  • I don’t keep or track your browsing history.
  • I don’t use analytics, ads or third-party services.
  • I don’t sell or share any data, for any reason.

The thank-you link

The extension has an optional link to PayPal. If you click it, you leave the extension and PayPal’s own privacy policy applies. I never see or handle your payment details.

Google’s user data policy

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
